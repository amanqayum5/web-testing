Sri Lanka Tax & Advisory Services

IRD-compliant tax planning, structuring, and advisory for domestic clients, foreign investors, and Sri Lankan expats living in the UK or Australia.

Our Expertise

Specialist Sri Lanka Tax Advice You Can Rely On

Whether you're a domestic business, a foreign investor entering the Sri Lankan market, or an expat managing assets between Colombo and London — LGTFA Ltd delivers clear, compliant, and structured tax advice tailored to Sri Lanka's unique regulatory landscape.

Sri Lanka's tax environment has undergone significant reform in recent years, with changes to income tax rates, VAT thresholds, and the treatment of remitted funds. Our advisors stay current with Inland Revenue Department (IRD) policy so you don't have to.

🏛️

IRD Compliance — Staying Ahead of Reform

Sri Lanka's Inland Revenue Act has seen major amendments affecting personal income tax brackets, corporate rates, and the exemption regime for foreign-sourced income. We ensure your filings reflect current law and avoid costly penalties.

What We Cover

📋

IRD Filings & Compliance

Corporate and individual income tax return preparation and lodgement with Sri Lanka's Inland Revenue Department — on time, every time.

Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)

💸

Property & Asset Tax Advisory

Capital gains, property transfer taxes, and withholding tax structures on Sri Lankan real estate and local asset disposals.

Corporate Tax Structuring

Advising Sri Lankan and foreign-owned companies on optimal corporate structures, deductible expenditure, and effective tax rate management.

Expat & Non-Resident Advisory

Tailored advice for Sri Lankan nationals living abroad and foreign nationals with income or assets in Sri Lanka — residency, withholding, and disclosure obligations.
