Sri Lanka Tax & Advisory Services

Page Header Banner Image

ABC provides specialized Sri Lankan tax advice to domestic clients, foreign investors, and Sri Lankan expats living in the UK or Australia. We advise on tax structuring, wealth protection, and Inland Revenue Department (IRD) compliance.

  • Inland Revenue Department (IRD) Filings: Corporate and individual income tax return preparation and lodgement.
  • Foreign Direct Investment (FDI): Strategic planning for overseas businesses establishing operations or entities in Sri Lanka.
  • Dual Resident & Remittance Advice: Tax handling on funds remitted between Sri Lanka, the UK, and Australia.
  • Property & Asset Tax Advisory: Capital gains, property transfer taxes, and withholding tax structures for local assets.
