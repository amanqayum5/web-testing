UK, Australia & Sri Lanka Cross-Border Tax Advice

Page Header Banner Image

ABC provides cross-border tax advice to individuals, businesses, trusts, and estates operating across the United Kingdom, Australia, and Sri Lanka. Managing wealth or commercial assets across multiple jurisdictions requires structured planning to prevent double taxation and ensure statutory compliance.

  • Double Taxation Agreements (DTA): Relief claims and structuring under the UK-Australia, UK-Sri Lanka, and Australia-Sri Lanka DTAs.
  • Residency & Expat Planning: Guidance on dual tax residency, statutory residence tests, and emigration or relocation tax implications.
  • Foreign Income & Capital Gains: Mitigating capital gains exposure on global assets, property, and offshore investments.
  • Cross-Border Estate & Trust Advisory: Managing foreign asset transfers, inheritance tax, and multi-country family trusts.
