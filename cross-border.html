Our Expertise

Managing Wealth and Compliance Across Borders

Managing wealth or commercial assets across multiple jurisdictions requires structured planning to prevent double taxation and ensure full statutory compliance with HMRC, the ATO, and Sri Lanka's Inland Revenue Department.

LGTFA Ltd provides cross-border tax advice to individuals, businesses, trusts, and estates operating across the United Kingdom, Australia, and Sri Lanka. Whether you're relocating internationally, managing offshore investments, or running a multi-jurisdiction business, our advisors provide clear, actionable guidance at every step.

Our team has deep expertise in the tax treaties that govern each jurisdiction pair — allowing us to structure affairs in a way that is both compliant and tax-efficient, avoiding the costly pitfalls of dual taxation.

What We Cover

📄

Double Taxation Agreements (DTAs)

Relief claims and cross-border structuring under the UK–Australia, UK–Sri Lanka, and Australia–Sri Lanka tax treaties.

🌍

Residency & Expat Planning

Guidance on dual tax residency, statutory residence tests, and the tax implications of emigration or international relocation.

📈

Foreign Income & Capital Gains

Mitigating capital gains exposure on global assets, overseas property, and offshore investment portfolios.

🏛️

Cross-Border Estate & Trust Advisory

Managing foreign asset transfers, multi-country inheritance tax obligations, and international family trust structures.

🏢

International Business Structuring

Advising on holding company structures, permanent establishment risks, and transfer pricing across UK, AUS, and Sri Lanka.

✅

Multi-Jurisdiction Compliance

Coordinated filing and disclosure obligations with HMRC, the ATO, and IRD — avoiding penalties and late filing charges.

Who This Service Is For

This service is designed for:

  • UK nationals living or working in Australia or Sri Lanka
  • Sri Lankan expats with income or assets in the UK or Australia
  • Businesses operating across more than one of these three jurisdictions
  • Trustees and executors administering multi-country estates
  • High-net-worth individuals with global investment portfolios
