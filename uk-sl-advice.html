UK and Sri Lanka Tax Advice

ABC Ltd provides dedicated tax advisory for individuals, dual-residents, and enterprises navigating the tax systems of both the United Kingdom and Sri Lanka.

Core Advisory Areas

  • UK-Sri Lanka Double Tax Agreement (DTA): Applying treaty benefits to prevent dual taxation on dividends, pensions, employment income, and foreign direct investment.
  • Residency & Non-Domicile Structuring: Guiding expats and dual-nationals on Statutory Residence Tests (SRT) in the UK and Inland Revenue Department (IRD) residence criteria in Sri Lanka.
  • Property & Asset Disposal: Capital Gains Tax (CGT) optimization and reporting for residential and commercial real estate held across both countries.
  • Repatriation of Wealth: Structuring compliant capital flows, fund transfers, and foreign currency account management between the UK and Sri Lanka.
