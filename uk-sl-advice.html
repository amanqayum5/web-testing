Our Expertise

Navigating Two Tax Systems With Confidence

The intersection of UK and Sri Lankan tax law creates a unique and often complex advisory landscape — one that requires deep knowledge of both HMRC requirements and Sri Lanka's Inland Revenue Department rules, as well as the treaty that governs their interaction.

LGTFA Ltd provides dedicated tax advisory for individuals, dual-residents, and enterprises navigating both tax systems simultaneously. Whether you're a Sri Lankan national living in the UK, a UK expatriate with assets in Colombo, or a business with operations spanning both countries, we deliver clarity and confidence.

🤝

The UK–Sri Lanka Double Taxation Agreement

The UK–Sri Lanka DTA determines how income, dividends, pensions, and capital gains are taxed when you have connections to both countries. Correctly applying treaty benefits can significantly reduce your overall tax burden — but requires expert interpretation and timely claims.

Your Obligations at a Glance

United Kingdom

HMRC obligations

  • Self-Assessment income tax returns
  • Capital Gains Tax on UK & overseas assets
  • Statutory Residence Test (SRT) determination
  • Non-dom remittance basis claims
  • Inheritance Tax (IHT) on worldwide estate
  • Overseas Workday Relief (OWR) claims

Sri Lanka

IRD obligations

  • Individual income tax return filings
  • Remittance-based income tax treatment
  • Property transfer & stamp duty
  • Withholding tax on dividends & interest
  • IRD residency status determination
  • Foreign asset declaration requirements
